At the first visit, the doctor listens to your concerns and wishes, examines you, and orders diagnostics if needed. We then discuss treatment options, timing, and an estimated cost. You receive a clear plan for the next steps with no obligation to start treatment immediately.

The timeline depends on the goal and method chosen. Professional hygiene usually takes about an hour, while orthodontic treatment can take several months. After diagnostics, the doctor will create an individual plan and set realistic timelines for your case.

This is an independent design concept. The consultation flow is a local preview: it does not send your details or create an appointment. Contact details will be added when a verified clinic is connected.
